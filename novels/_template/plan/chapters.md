# Chapters

One row per chapter, ten to fifteen planned ahead of the last accepted chapter, never the whole
book. temp: fast, tense, loud, warm, funny, bleak, procedural or quiet. hook (how it ends):
reveal, arrival, decision, question, threat, reversal, cliff or quiet. threads: `~T1` opens,
`^T1` advances, `vT1` pays, `xT1` drops.
