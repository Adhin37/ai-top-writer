# People, objects and figures

The last blocks' `at`, `has` and `cost` lines say where everyone ended up, who holds the objects
that matter, and what is still hurting. The cast files say what someone looks like, what they can do
and how they speak, and a walk-on's one line in `bible/cast/_extras.md` is as binding as a full
profile. The bible's figures (prices, wages, distances, durations, counts) keep their meaning: a
price is never reused as a length of time.

## Patterns

### In two places

> C0004 at: "Ossie: the canteen" · Draft (the next morning, first scene): "Ossie was already at the
> pump hall when she arrived."

That is fine if the draft moves him, and a finding if it does not and the chapter's first lines treat
him as never having left.

### In two hands

```
F3 state  "turned the boathouse key in the lock" | C0003 has: "Orrin: the boathouse key" | she gave it to Orrin last chapter
```

### Recovered too fast

```
F4 state  "climbed the ladder two rungs at a time" | C0003 cost: "no grip in her left hand for days" | one day later
```

### A first appearance that contradicts the file

> Draft: "the recruiter, a young man with a clerk's soft hands"
> bible/cast/_extras.md: "Recruiter — Deck Seven, deep crews — older woman, signs chits without
> looking"

The first time someone is placed on the page is the reader's picture for the rest of the book.

### A figure reused

> bible/society.md: "a canteen meal costs 2 credits" · Draft: "two credits of air would see him
> through the shift"

The same figure now means air. Check every number the draft uses against the line in the bible that
defines it, and the unit beside it.

### A body that changed and stayed changed

When a character's body or form has changed (an injury that will not heal, a transformation, a
second body), the bible or the last block says what they look like now. A draft that describes the
old body is a finding.

## When it does not apply

A new detail the bible does not hold (a scar no file mentions, a price never set) is not a finding.
The clerk lists it and the planner folds it in. Report only what contradicts.
