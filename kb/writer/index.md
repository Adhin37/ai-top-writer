# Writer — knowledge base

Start with [prompt.md](prompt.md) — your procedure.

| doc | open it when |
|---|---|
| [orienting-the-reader.md](orienting-the-reader.md) | drafting chapter 1, or any chapter whose beat sheet lists something the reader must learn — how to put a world's rules on the page plainly, at the moment they bite |
| [scene-and-summary.md](scene-and-summary.md) | deciding what to play in full and what to bridge — the event gets the scene |
| [scenes.md](scenes.md) | building a scene: a want, an opposition, a changed exit; three or more speakers; somebody has to give ground |
| [dialogue.md](dialogue.md) | writing any conversation — spoken, not recited |
| [distinct-voices.md](distinct-voices.md) | a scene with two or more speakers — keeping each one their own mind, from the cast sheet |
| [narration.md](narration.md) | always, once per chapter — distance, thought without tags or filters, other minds through their first move |
| [world-on-the-page.md](world-on-the-page.md) | a place, a custom or a rule of the world is on the page — handled, paid for, worked around |
| [intelligence-on-the-page.md](intelligence-on-the-page.md) | someone deduces, plans, lies or misreads a person |
| [openings-and-endings.md](openings-and-endings.md) | always — the first lines and the last, and the beat sheet's `hook` |
| [../shared/wire.md](../shared/wire.md) | writing the facts file and your final line — ids, short quotes, one item per line |
| [../shared/format-spec.md](../shared/format-spec.md) | writing the file — channels, viewpoint, numbers, spellings, frontmatter |

**Optional modules.** Open one only when `novel.md` switches it on:

| doc | `novel.md` key | open it when |
|---|---|---|
| [toggle-combat.md](toggle-combat.md) | `optional.combat-choreography: on` | the chapter has a fight |
| [toggle-battle-scale.md](toggle-battle-scale.md) | `optional.battle-scale: on` | an engagement too big for anyone to see whole |
| [toggle-comedy.md](toggle-comedy.md) | `optional.comedy-levity: on` | the beat sheet places a comic beat, or a character with wit is on the page |
| [toggle-slice-of-life.md](toggle-slice-of-life.md) | `optional.slice-of-life-texture: on` | always, when on — work, food, money and small rituals inside the scenes |
| [toggle-grimdark.md](toggle-grimdark.md) | `optional.grimdark-consequences: on` | a death, an injury or a moral cost is on the page |
| [toggle-pov-switch.md](toggle-pov-switch.md) | `pov.switch_granularity` other than `never` | the chapter or a scene is in another viewpoint |
