# Bias at the line level

**This is the one area whose rules are absolute.** They override genre convention, a user's tropes
and any reference material. Prejudice can be depicted, examined and suffered; what may not happen
is prejudice operating unexamined as the narration's own view. The test for any sentence: could the
narrator's framing be quoted as a bigoted statement with nothing lost? If so, fix the framing, not
the character. The structural half (who the world rewards, who is competent, factions) is the
planner's.

## The rules

- **No physical marker signals moral quality.** Not skin, features, hair, build, scars or
  disability; watch the quiet version, the villain's darkness and the ally's fairness as habit.
- **No beauty catalogue.** A woman is introduced by what she is doing, as a man would be, never by
  an inventory of her body.
- **No accent as stupidity or villainy, and no phonetic dialect spelling.** Origin is carried by
  rhythm and idiom.
- **No body, age, class or job as a punchline**, and no epithet that does a group's moral work
  ("the greasy merchant", "the shifty foreigner").
- **Interiority is not rationed by group.** If only some kinds of people get a thought on the page,
  that is the narration's view.

## Patterns

**The epithet doing the work:**

> The scarred deputy smiled, and Tovi knew at once he was lying.

**The framing fixed, with the character unchanged:**

> The deputy smiled, and kept smiling a beat too long after she'd stopped talking. Tovi knew that
> smile; she'd used it on supervisors.

The deputy can still be lying. The scar no longer tells the reader so.

**The catalogue:**

> The recruiter was a tall woman with full lips, long dark hair and a figure her uniform did little
> to hide.

**Introduced by action:**

> The recruiter was signing chits without looking at them, four to the minute, and she didn't look
> up for Tovi either.

## When it does not apply

A character may hold a prejudice and say it, and the story may show it costing someone. That is
depiction, and it stays. Check only that the narration around it does not agree.
