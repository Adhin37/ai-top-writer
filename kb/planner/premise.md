# The premise sheet

The bible is everything that is true. The premise is the part of it a reader must **hold** by the
end of chapter 1 to follow anything that happens after: what is different about this world and
how it works, who the protagonist is and what they want, and what stands in the way. Three to seven
facts. Written in plain words, because the test of each is whether a reader could say it back.

A fact names a **mechanism**, not a label. "The Assay decides everything" is a label; "every coin
in the city is weighed at the Assay, and a family whose coin comes up light loses its trading
licence for a year" is a mechanism — a reader could predict something from it.

## Example

The world is invented.

```markdown
---
type: reference
title: Premise
---
# Premise

## In one breath
On the Ansel Stack, a mining habitat with no outside, the air everyone breathes is metered and
paid for by their job grade. Tovi Brand, a pipe-fitter who has just been stripped of her grade,
has thirty days of air on credit to find work or be shipped down-well — and the only crews hiring
are the ones nobody comes back from.

## Load-bearing facts
| id | fact, in plain words | source |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | The Stack is sealed: there is no outside, and all air comes from the processors on Deck Nine | bible/world.md §Physical |
| P2 | Every person's air is metered and billed each shift; your job grade decides how much of it is paid for | bible/world.md §Rules 1 |
| P3 | Lose your grade and you get thirty days of air on credit; on day thirty-one you are shipped down-well | bible/society.md §Law |
| P4 | Tovi Brand, a pipe-fitter, has just lost her grade — for a fault that was not hers | novel.md mc |
| P5 | The only work open to the ungraded is the deep crews, and the deep crews do not come back | bible/society.md §Labour |
```

## Checks

- **Say it back.** Read "in one breath" aloud. Could a friend repeat it after one hearing? If a
  sentence needs a coined term, gloss it in the same sentence or replace it.
- **Every fact cites a source.** A fact with no source is invented; if the premise needs it, it
  goes into the bible first, and the hand-back says so.
- **Not too much.** Faction politics, history, the magic system's third rule — these are true, and
  they are chapter 5's business. The premise is only what chapter 1 cannot do without.
