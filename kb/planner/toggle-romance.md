# A romantic line

A romance is a plot: two people want something, are obstructed, and change. It is not a reward
schedule attached to the main story. The lead is designed **after** the protagonist, as a
counterweight to that specific person, with a goal of their own that costs them something to pursue
alongside the relationship. "Decide later" is often the best configuration: a lead chosen at the
first arc boundary from people who already exist is usually better than one designed in advance.
With `optional.no-harem: on` there is one active romantic line at a time, attraction is motivated
and reciprocal or it is a story about rejection, and a rejection holds.

## Examples

### A reason, not a description

**Unmotivated:**

> Ossie is drawn to Tovi because she is beautiful and strong.

**Motivated:**

> Ossie is drawn to Tovi because she is the only grade-holder who ever told him the real return
> count instead of the comforting one. Tovi is drawn to him because he has never once asked her for
> anything she could not afford.

### The beats, with the real obstacle

| beat | for this pair | about when |
|---|---|---|
| meet | a first impression that is wrong in an interesting way: she takes him for a liability | early |
| friction | he wants to stay on the Stack; she is going down-well | continuous |
| alliance | they need each other for something not romantic: the stores ledger | about a quarter in |
| vulnerability | he shows her the debt he hides; she does not use it | about two fifths |
| obstacle | the real one: she leads the crew that his brother died on | past the middle |

The obstacle is values, loyalty or circumstance, never a misunderstanding a conversation would
solve.

### The agency test

What does the lead want that is not the protagonist? If the only answer is the protagonist, the
lead is a reward and not a person. Rewrite the lead's goal before planning a single beat.

## When to break it

A novel with `content.romance: none` has no romance, and needs none. Do not add one because the genre
usually has one.
