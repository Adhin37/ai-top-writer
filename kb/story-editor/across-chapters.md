# Across chapters

A chapter can pass every test on its own and still be the fourth in a row with the same shape.
`state/scenes.md` shows the last chapters' scenes: who was in them, where, at what tempo, and
whether they were two people talking. The reader's memory (`reading/<id>/notes.md`) shows what the
reader believes and expects. Sameness and drift are notes for the planner when they are not yet a
cost to this chapter, and notes for the writer when they already are.

## Patterns

### Two people talking, again

```
| 3 | 2 | Tovi, Kell      | shift office  | tense | yes |
| 4 | 1 | Tovi, Ossie     | the canteen   | quiet | yes |
| 4 | 2 | Tovi, recruiter | Deck Seven    | tense | yes |
```

Three conversations in a row, each between Tovi and one other person. Each scene works alone. A
reader feels the run as the story standing still. If this draft is the third, the note for the
writer is to give one scene a third party with business of its own, or something to do with
their hands besides talk. The note for the planner is the run itself.

### One tempo

Five chapters at `tense` read as one long chapter. The scene log shows the tempo, and the plan row's
`temp` shows what was meant. If they disagree, the draft played it differently from the plan.

### The reader's belief has drifted

The reader's memory holds something the page never said:

> Notes: "Ossie (dead?) — hasn't appeared since ch. 3"

If the plan needs Ossie alive and absent, one line of evidence in a later chapter fixes it. That is
a *For the planner* line, with the reader's words.

### The prediction this chapter could pay

The reader's memory predicts something this chapter is about to do: they will feel it land. If
the draft does it offstage, see [promises.md](promises.md).

### The recurring gesture

The same small move in three chapters (the hand to the pocket, the folded paper, the last line that
withholds) is the line editor's to thin. Mention it only when it carries a scene's turn.

## When it does not apply

A deliberate run is a choice the plan says it is making: a stretch of quiet chapters before a
climax, or one interrogation after another because the story is an investigation. The plan rows
and the beat sheet will say so. Then the sameness is the shape.
