# The delivery test

Five questions, one sentence each, none strained. **Want:** what was the viewpoint character after?
**Friction:** what person, rule or scarcity was in the way? **Change:** what is materially different
at the end? **Cost:** what was paid, and by whom? **Next:** what does the reader now need to see?
Change carries the load, and it is not a summary of events.

## Patterns

### The change is a feeling

> The answer to *Change* is "Tovi realises the system is against her."

That is an interior weather report, and it would fit any chapter. A change is something you could
state without narrating the chapter: "Tovi is now someone the shift office watches on purpose", or
"the slip is signed". If only a feeling changed, ask what happened on the page to cause it.

```
N1 the chapter changes how she feels, not where she stands
where "She knew now that the Stack had never been on her side."
ev    own reading: no exit state differs from the entry; the slip is still unsigned, Kell still unchallenged
eff   the reader finishes with nothing to carry into chapter 5
```

### The event, reported

> "By the time the bell rang, she had argued Kell down and won the extra shift."

The plan row's event happened in a past-perfect clause. The longest scene is somewhere else, usually
a bridge (the walk, the wait). This is always a note, and the evidence is the event's own line in
the beat sheet against the quote.

### Friction that is only hesitation

The only thing between her and what she wants is her own doubt, for the second chapter running. A
person, a rule or a scarcity with its own reason is the fix; doubt can sit beside it.

### The repeat that does not escalate

A second refusal costs exactly what the first did. Either the second costs more, or one is cut.

### The next that is already answered

The chapter ends on a question the chapter itself answered three pages earlier. The reader turns
the page with nothing to want.

## When it does not apply

A chapter built as aftermath (the morning after a death) may have a small want and a quiet change.
It still has one: someone decides something. If nobody does, it is a scene from the next chapter
that came loose.
