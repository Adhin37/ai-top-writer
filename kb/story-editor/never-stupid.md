# Nobody made stupid for the plot

When the protagonist fails, the reader must be able to see why: they lacked information, someone
at least as capable wanted the opposite and moved first, they saw the answer and could not afford
it, or they misread a person in the way their profile says they do. Never because they forgot
what they know. And nobody else is made slow so the protagonist can look quick.

## Patterns

### The forgotten fact

> Chapter 2: Tovi learns the recruiter lies about return rates. Chapter 4: she believes his numbers
> and signs.

She knew. Unless the page gives a reason she would sign anyway (no other offer, her brother's air,
the lie priced in), this is the author lowering her to reach the plot.

### The deduction from nowhere

She concludes something that no line on the page gave her. Name the sentence it came from; if
there is none, the conclusion is the author's.

### The convenient fool

> The supervisor, who has run this deck for twenty years, does not notice the forged chit.

The opposition is made blind so the protagonist can win. Opposition that is competent and still
loses, because it lacked one fact or paid one cost, is the version a reader believes.

### The narrator vouching

> Tovi, the sharpest fitter on the deck, saw through it at once.

A label instead of a demonstration. That is a line note, not a story note, unless the label is
doing the work a scene should have done.

### Another mind stated as fact

> Kell had already decided to refuse her.

In a limited viewpoint, nobody knows that. The repair is one clause: the evidence she reads it from.

### Knowing the future and not planning

A protagonist with foreknowledge who forgets to use it is the forgotten fact in another form. The
tension comes from an incomplete map, never from a dulled mind.

### The gap that never moves

The distance between the protagonist and the opposition is the same at the end of the chapter as
at the start, for the third chapter running. Something should widen it or close it.

## When it does not apply

A character exhausted, grieving or outside their trade may reason badly. That is a cause the page
shows. If it shows it, there is no note.
